# 周公解梦 (周公解梦)

## 概述

本技能使 WorkBuddy 能够基于中国传统「周公解梦」经典方法论，结合现代心理象征学，提供梦境解析。将梦境描述转化为有意义的象征解读，涵盖运势、生活事件、情绪及潜意识信号。

## 何时使用本技能

在以下情况使用本技能：
- 用户描述了一个梦并询问其含义
- 用户提到「周公解梦」、「解梦」、「dream interpretation」
- 用户询问「梦见...是什么意思」或类似表述
- 用户想了解梦中元素的象征意义
- 用户提到特定梦境符号（飞行、坠落、水、动物等）并询问其意义

## 解梦工作流

### 第 1 步：收集梦境细节

请用户尽可能详细地描述他们的梦。需要识别的关键要素：
- **主要人物/实体**：梦中出现了谁或什么？
- **场景/地点**：梦发生在哪里？
- **动作/事件**：发生了什么？梦者在做什么？
- **物品/对象**：出现了哪些重要物品？
- **情绪**：梦者在梦中及醒来后感觉如何？
- **颜色**：有任何突出的颜色吗？
- **数字**：有任何特别的数字吗？

### 第 2 步：识别核心象征

将梦拆解为独立的象征元素。梦中的每个元素都携带潜在意义：
- **自然元素**：水、火、土、风、山、河
- **动物**：不同动物携带不同的传统意义
- **人物**：家庭成员、陌生人、名人、历史人物
- **物品**：工具、车辆、建筑、食物、衣物
- **动作**：飞行、坠落、奔跑、打斗、拥抱
- **场景**：家、工作场所、学校、未知场所、自然

### 第 3 步：应用解读框架

对每个象征符号，应用分层解读：

1. **中国传统解读**（周公解梦经典含义）
2. **心理象征学**（潜意识欲望、恐惧、焦虑）
3. **生活情境**（可能相关的当前生活状况）
4. **情绪共鸣**（梦所唤起的感受）

### 第 4 步：综合意义

将各个象征的解读合成为连贯的叙事：
- 识别梦的**主导主题**
- 注意可能暗示内心冲突的**矛盾象征**
- 考虑**整体情绪基调**（积极、消极、焦虑、平和）
- 提供**具体象征意义**和**整体梦境解读**

### 第 5 步：交付解读

以结构化格式呈现解读：

```
## 梦境解析 (Dream Interpretation)

### 梦境概述 (Dream Summary)
[梦境简要总结]

### 核心象征解析 (Core Symbol Analysis)
- **象征 1**：传统含义 + 心理含义
- **象征 2**：传统含义 + 心理含义
- ...

### 整体运势 (Overall Fortune)
[总体指示：吉/凶/参半，附解释]

### 生活启示 (Life Insights)
[关于当前生活状况的实用洞察]

### 建议 (Recommendations)
[基于梦境的可操作建议]
```

- **未知场所**：探索自我的未知方面

- **地下/地下室**：潜意识、隐藏方面、秘密

## 解梦原则

1. **情境决定一切**：同一象征根据梦者的生活状况可能意味着不同事物
2. **情绪真实比字面真实更重要**：梦的感觉往往比发生了什么更重要
3. **个人联想优先于通用含义**：如果某个象征具有个人意义，以它为准
4. **反复出现的梦指示重要信息**：特别注意重复的梦
5. **梦者是最好的解梦者**：提供指引，但赋予用户找到自己意义的能力
6. **平衡传统与心理学**：将中国经典解读与现代心理洞察结合
7. **避免宿命论**：将解读框定为洞察和可能性，而非固定预言

## 重要注意事项

- 梦是高度个人化的；解读应作为可能性呈现，而非绝对真理
- 解读时考虑梦者当前的生活状况
- 尊重关于梦的文化和个人信仰
- 避免因负面解读引起不必要的焦虑；将挑战框定为机遇
- 当梦包含多个矛盾象征时，探索这可能代表的内心冲突
- 如果梦特别令人不安，建议梦者可能与心理健康专业人士交谈会有帮助

## English

### Overview

This skill enables WorkBuddy to provide traditional Chinese dream interpretation based on the classical "Zhou Gong's Dream Dictionary" (周公解梦) methodology, combined with modern psychological symbolism. It transforms dream descriptions into meaningful symbolic interpretations covering fortune, life events, emotions, and subconscious signals.

### When to Use This Skill

Use this skill when:
- The user describes a dream and asks for its meaning
- The user mentions "周公解梦", "解梦", "dream interpretation"
- The user asks "梦见...是什么意思" or similar phrasing
- The user wants to understand symbolic meanings of dream elements
- The user mentions specific dream symbols (flying, falling, water, animals, etc.) and asks about significance

### Dream Interpretation Workflow

#### Step 1: Gather Dream Details

Ask the user to describe their dream with as much detail as possible. Key elements to identify:
- **Main characters/entities**: Who or what appeared in the dream?
- **Setting/location**: Where did the dream take place?
- **Actions/events**: What happened? What was the dreamer doing?
- **Objects/items**: Significant objects that appeared
- **Emotions**: How did the dreamer feel during and after the dream?
- **Colors**: Any prominent colors?
- **Numbers**: Any specific numbers that stood out?

#### Step 2: Identify Core Symbols

Break down the dream into individual symbolic elements. Each element in a dream carries potential meaning:
- **Natural elements**: Water, fire, earth, wind, mountains, rivers
- **Animals**: Different animals carry distinct traditional meanings
- **People**: Family members, strangers, celebrities, historical figures
- **Objects**: Tools, vehicles, buildings, food, clothing
- **Actions**: Flying, falling, running, fighting, embracing
- **Settings**: Home, workplace, school, unknown places, nature

#### Step 3: Apply Interpretation Framework

For each symbol, apply layered interpretation:

1. **Traditional Chinese interpretation** (周公解梦 classical meaning)
2. **Psychological symbolism** (subconscious desires, fears, anxieties)
3. **Life context** (current life situations that might relate)
4. **Emotional resonance** (feelings the dream evokes)

#### Step 4: Synthesize Meaning

Combine individual symbol interpretations into a coherent narrative:
- Identify the **dominant theme** of the dream
- Note any **conflicting symbols** that might indicate internal conflict
- Consider the **overall emotional tone** (positive, negative, anxious, peaceful)
- Provide both **specific symbol meanings** and **holistic dream interpretation**

#### Step 5: Deliver Interpretation

Present the interpretation in a structured format:

```
## Dream Interpretation

### Dream Summary
[Brief summary of the dream]

### Core Symbol Analysis
- **Symbol 1**: Traditional meaning + psychological meaning
- **Symbol 2**: Traditional meaning + psychological meaning
- ...

### Overall Fortune
[General indication: auspicious/inauspicious/mixed, with explanation]

### Life Insights
[Practical insights about current life situations]

### Recommendations
[Actionable advice based on the dream]
```

### Interpretation Principles

1. **Context is everything**: The same symbol can mean different things depending on the dreamer's life situation
2. **Emotional truth matters more than literal truth**: How the dream felt is often more important than what happened
3. **Personal associations override general meanings**: If a symbol has personal significance, that takes precedence
4. **Recurring dreams indicate important messages**: Pay special attention to dreams that repeat
5. **The dreamer is the best interpreter**: Provide guidance, but empower the user to find their own meaning
6. **Balance tradition with psychology**: Use classical Chinese interpretations alongside modern psychological insights
7. **Avoid deterministic fortune-telling**: Frame interpretations as insights and possibilities, not fixed predictions

### Important Notes

- Dreams are highly personal; interpretations should be presented as possibilities, not absolute truths
- Consider the dreamer's current life circumstances when interpreting
- Be respectful of cultural and personal beliefs about dreams
- Avoid causing unnecessary anxiety with negative interpretations; frame challenges as opportunities
- When a dream contains multiple conflicting symbols, explore the internal conflict this might represent
- If a dream is particularly disturbing, suggest the dreamer might benefit from speaking with a mental health professional
