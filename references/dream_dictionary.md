# Dream Symbol Dictionary (解梦象征词典)

## A
- **Airplane/Flying**: Career advancement, freedom, desire to escape current situation. Auspicious for ambitions.
- **Animals (general)**: Instincts, natural urges, aspects of personality. Context determines meaning.
- **Apple**: Temptation, knowledge, health, desire. Golden apple = wealth; rotten apple = missed opportunity.
- **Armor**: Protection, defensiveness, preparing for battle, emotional walls.

## B
- **Baby**: New beginnings, innocence, vulnerable aspects of self, creative projects.
- **Balloon**: Inflated hopes, temporary happiness, fragility of plans.
- **Beach**: Transition between conscious and subconscious, relaxation, emotional boundary.
- **Bear**: Strength, hibernation/introspection, motherly protection, or hidden aggression.
- **Bed**: Rest, intimacy, vulnerability, private self.
- **Bicycle**: Balance, self-propelled progress, simplicity, personal effort.
- **Bird**: Freedom, aspiration, spiritual messages. Specific types carry additional meanings.
- **Biting**: Aggression, needing to defend oneself, verbal attacks, holding on too tightly.
- **Blood**: Life force, vitality, family bonds, sacrifice, or injury/loss.
- **Boat**: Life's journey, emotional navigation, transition. Calm water = smooth sailing.
- **Book**: Knowledge, wisdom, secrets, life story. Open book = clarity; closed book = mystery.
- **Box**: Hidden aspects, containment, gifts, secrets waiting to be discovered.
- **Bridge**: Transition, connection, overcoming obstacles, moving from one phase to another.
- **Building**: Self-structure, ambitions, different aspects of personality based on building type.
- **Butterfly**: Transformation, beauty, fleeting joy, soul, rebirth.

## C
- **Cage**: Restriction, trapped feelings, desire for freedom, self-imposed limitations.
- **Cake**: Rewards, celebrations, indulgence, desire for sweetness in life.
- **Car**: Control over life direction, status, personal drive. Who's driving matters.
- **Cat**: Independence, intuition, mystery, feminine energy, cunning.
- **Cave**: Subconscious depths, hidden fears, seeking shelter, introspection.
- **Celebration**: Social connections, achievements, need for joy, community.
- **Chain**: Bondage, obligations, connections, things that bind or restrict.
- **Chair**: Position, status, rest, authority. Empty chair = absence or opportunity.
- **Chase/Being Chased**: Avoiding problems, running from fears, unresolved issues pursuing you.
- **Child**: Innocence, new beginnings, vulnerable self, creative potential.
- **Church/Temple**: Spirituality, seeking guidance, moral questions, sanctuary.
- **City**: Social environment, complexity, opportunities, feeling overwhelmed or energized.
- **Cliff**: Danger, precipice of decision, risk, boundary between safety and danger.
- **Climbing**: Ambition, effort toward goals, overcoming obstacles, striving.
- **Clock**: Time pressure, mortality, schedules, awareness of passing time.
- **Clothes**: Self-presentation, identity, social mask, how you want to be seen.
- **Clouds**: Confusion, temporary states, imagination, messages from the unconscious.
- **Color**: Each color carries specific meaning (see Colors section).
- **Comb/Hairbrush**: Ordering thoughts, grooming self-image, attention to appearance.
- **Computer**: Logic, communication, work, modern stress, information processing.
- **Cooking**: Creativity, nurturing, preparation, transforming raw materials.
- **Corpse**: Endings, transformation, fear of mortality, something that needs to be laid to rest.
- **Crown**: Achievement, authority, recognition, highest aspirations.
- **Crystal**: Clarity, purity, spiritual insight, fragility, value.

## D
- **Dance**: Harmony, expression, joy, relationship dynamics, freedom of movement.
- **Darkness**: Unknown, fear, unconscious, mystery, ignorance, potential.
- **Dawn**: New beginnings, hope, enlightenment, fresh starts.
- **Death**: Transformation, endings, fear of change, letting go, major life transition.
- **Demon**: Inner shadow, repressed desires, guilt, fears, destructive impulses.
- **Desert**: Isolation, spiritual testing, barrenness, purification, emptiness.
- **Diamond**: Clarity, value, endurance, commitment, precious aspects of self.
- **Dinosaur**: Outdated attitudes, ancient fears, something from the past returning.
- **Doctor**: Healing, need for care, authority on health, problem-solving.
- **Dog**: Loyalty, friendship, protection, instinct, trustworthiness.
- **Door**: Opportunity, transition, choices, boundaries. Open = welcome; closed = obstacle.
- **Dragon (Chinese)**: Power, success, authority, imperial fortune, masculine energy. Extremely auspicious.
- **Drowning**: Overwhelmed by emotions, losing control, being consumed by unconscious fears.
- **Dust**: Neglect, decay, forgotten matters, things that have settled, cleaning needed.

## E
- **Eagle**: Vision, freedom, power, spiritual elevation, perspective from above.
- **Earthquake**: Major upheaval, foundational shift, crisis, releasing pent-up energy.
- **Eating**: Nourishment, taking in experiences, greed, sensual pleasure, need for fulfillment.
- **Egg**: Potential, new beginnings, fertility, fragility, creativity waiting to hatch.
- **Elephant**: Memory, wisdom, strength, stability, overcoming obstacles.
- **Elevator**: Rise or fall in status, consciousness levels, life transitions.
- **Embrace**: Acceptance, love, reconciliation, need for connection.
- **Exam/Test**: Self-evaluation, performance anxiety, feeling unprepared, life challenges.
- **Eye**: Perception, awareness, judgment, truth, being watched, self-examination.

## F
- **Fairy**: Magic, childlike wonder, desire for enchantment, escapism.
- **Falling**: Loss of control, fear of failure, insecurity, letting go, descending into unconscious.
- **Family**: Aspects of self, roots, inheritance, unresolved dynamics, support system.
- **Farm**: Hard work, productivity, natural cycles, self-sufficiency.
- **Father**: Authority, protection, discipline, wisdom, relationship with power.
- **Feather**: Lightness, spiritual ascent, communication, truth, fragility.
- **Fence**: Boundaries, protection, limitations, keeping things in or out.
- **Festival**: Celebration, community, abundance, joy, cultural connection.
- **Fire**: Passion, anger, transformation, destruction, purification, career energy.
- **Fish**: Abundance, wealth, fertility, Christianity (symbol), opportunities. Swimming freely = good fortune.
- **Flag**: Identity, territory, beliefs, signals, rallying point.
- **Flood**: Overwhelming emotions, loss of control, purification, major life changes.
- **Flower**: Beauty, growth, fleeting nature of life, sexuality, specific types have meanings.
- **Flying**: Aspiration, freedom, escape, success, spiritual elevation.
- **Fog**: Confusion, uncertainty, hidden truths, temporary obscurity.
- **Food**: Nourishment, pleasure, abundance, social bonding, basic needs.
- **Forest**: Unknown territory, exploration, natural growth, being lost, finding oneself.
- **Fountain**: Emotional release, abundance, purification, source of life.
- **Fox**: Cleverness, cunning, adaptability, deception, beauty.
- **Friend**: Aspects of self, support, social needs, qualities you value.
- **Frog**: Transformation, fertility, cleansing, adapting to environment.
- **Funeral**: Endings, letting go, respect for the past, mourning necessary losses.

## G
- **Garden**: Growth, fertility, cultivated self, paradise, nurturing aspects.
- **Gate**: Threshold, opportunity, barrier, transition point, choices.
- **Ghost**: Past trauma, unresolved issues, fears, memory, things that haunt you.
- **Gift**: Recognition, unexpected benefits, talents, self-worth, relationships.
- **Glass**: Fragility, transparency, barriers, clarity, breakability.
- **Gold**: Wealth, value, highest quality, success, solar energy, divine connection.
- **Grass**: Natural growth, humility, vast numbers, covering truth, foundation.
- **Grave/Graveyard**: Endings, fear of death, unresolved grief, secrets buried.

## H

- **Hand**: Action, capability, connection, power, giving or receiving. Injured hand = feeling incapable.
- **Hat**: Role, identity, thoughts, status. Changing hats = changing roles.
- **Heart**: Love, emotion, vitality, courage, core self.
- **Heaven**: Spiritual aspirations, ideal state, wish fulfillment, peace.

- **Hill**: Lesser obstacle, vantage point, effort required, gradual climb.
- **Home**: Self, security, identity, inner world, family dynamics.
- **Horse**: Career, energy, travel, vitality, success, freedom, power.
- **Hospital**: Healing, vulnerability, need for care, fixing what's broken.

## Traditional Chinese Auspicious Dreams (吉梦)
- Dreaming of dragons, phoenixes, or other auspicious creatures
- Clear water, especially flowing toward the dreamer
- Climbing mountains successfully
- Receiving gifts, especially jade or gold
- Blooming flowers, especially peonies or lotuses
- Bright sun or moon
- Flying freely and joyfully
- Eating sweet fruits
- Finding treasure
- Receiving blessings from elders or deities

## Traditional Chinese Inauspicious Dreams (凶梦)
- Falling into dirty water or mud
- Teeth falling out (especially many teeth)
- Being chased by terrifying creatures
- House collapsing or on fire
- Losing important items
- Being trapped or imprisoned
- Death of close family members (may indicate worry rather than actual death)
- Darkness with no way out
- Breaking mirrors or jade
- Being naked in public with shame
